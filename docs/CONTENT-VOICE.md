# Website voice and headline contract

This is an independent senior-engineering consultancy with a distinctive voice, not a corporate brochure.

## Headlines

- Lead with attitude and economy. Think an 80s action-film one-liner: brief, confident, occasionally humorous.
- Prefer short, specific sentences, contrast, and a memorable rhythm. No buzzword chains.
- Avoid em dashes. Use periods, commas, or colons.
- Do not turn every label into a joke. Functional labels for navigation, forms, privacy and technical case studies should remain literal and accessible.
- Never substitute swagger for evidence. Do not promise guaranteed business outcomes, perfection, cost savings or technical certainty without substantiation.
- Keep the client's problem and offered work legible in the supporting paragraph, service details and page metadata.

## Example pattern

Headline: **Old systems. New tricks.**

Supporting description: Turn fragile legacy platforms and isolated data into maintainable services and tools that teams can actually use.

The headline creates interest; the supporting copy provides clarity.

## Consistency

- Canonical shared copy is in `public/data/consulting.json`.
- React page headings and the crawler HTML publisher in `scripts/generate-static-pages.mjs` must reflect the same page-level message.
- Preserve the name **Bytes** for writing.
- Keep project case-study titles, evidence, role attribution and technical contracts precise. Personality belongs primarily in positioning and section headings.
